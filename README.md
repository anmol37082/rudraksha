3. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Subject to the final approved quotation and manpower plan, the Service Provider may provide the following services:

A. HOSPITALITY & GUEST MANAGEMENT : 
Professional and well-groomed hospitality staff responsible for managing guest check-ins and check-outs, registration desks, and room allotment coordination. The team ensures a seamless guest experience by providing guidance and assistance, communicating event schedules, addressing guest queries, and coordinating efficiently with the Planner, host, hotel team, and relevant vendors to ensure smooth event operations.
B. INVITATIONS & RSVP COORDINATION (if expressly included in the approved scope) : 
Managing digital invitations and sending timely reminders on behalf of the host, tracking guest RSVPs, including confirmations and declines, and collecting travel details. The team also handles post-confirmation guest queries in coordination with the host and Planner, while preparing preliminary rooming lists and transportation sheets to ensure smooth guest management and seamless event coordination.
C. LOGISTICS & TRANSPORT COORDINATION :

Preparing and regularly updating transportation sheets, allocating vehicles as per the Planner’s approval, and tracking guests’ flight and train arrivals and departures. Coordinating guest welcomes and pickups at airports, railway stations, and bus terminals, assisting with luggage handling, and promptly reporting any vehicle, driver, or transportation-related issues to the Planner to ensure smooth and timely guest movement throughout the event. 


D. EVENT SUPPORT : 
Providing comprehensive event coordination and on-ground support, including guest shadow assistance, logistics management, facilitation services, welcome hostesses, food and beverage (F&B) coordination, butler services, and security-related manpower, strictly as per the Planner’s specific requirements and subject to prior written approval.
The scope of services shall be strictly limited to the number of personnel and duties specified in the final approved event confirmation. The Service Provider shall not be held responsible for any services, responsibilities, or additional requirements that have not been mutually agreed upon in writing.

5. SHIFT HOURS & OVERTIME
Unless otherwise specified in the approved quotation, the standard shift duration shall be 12 hours. Welcome Hostess shifts shall be 8 hours, while Butlers and Gunmen/ASO personnel shall be charged on a per-event basis, as specified in the rate card.
For a 12-hour shift, an additional charge equivalent to half a shift shall apply for the next 4 hours, followed by another half-shift charge for the subsequent 4 hours. Any extension beyond the approved shift duration must be authorized by the Planner or their designated on-site representative. All additional charges shall be calculated in accordance with the mutually agreed rate card or quotation.

Unless expressly included in the approved quotation, the following expenses shall be excluded from the service fees and shall be borne or reimbursed by the Planner at actual cost, subject to reasonable supporting details: staff meals, accommodation, travel, laundry, vehicle or cab fares, tolls, parking, taxes and entry tickets, sourcing or purchase costs, and rental charges for walkie-talkies or other communication equipment.

The Service Provider shall not be held responsible for any delays, disruptions, or gaps in service arising from incomplete or inaccurate information, delayed approvals, venue restrictions, vendor-related issues, transportation operator delays or failures, or any other circumstances beyond the Service Provider’s reasonable control.

B. Cancellation by the Planner :

After commencement of guest invitations/RSVP work : 50% of the total agreed bill amount shall be retained, and the balance refunded within 15 days of receiving written cancellation notice.
After completion of guest invitations/RSVP work : 100% of the total agreed bill amount shall be retained.
